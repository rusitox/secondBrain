# Product Vision: AI Chief of Staff / Digital Twin

## Overview
An advanced AI assistant designed to act as a Digital Twin and Chief of Staff. The system ingests, stores, and analyzes all professional interactions to provide proactive assistance, manage pending tasks, and eventually automate workflows by learning the user's behavior and communication style.

## Core Capabilities

### 1. Ingestion & Memory (The Brain)
- **Omnichannel Data Ingestion:** Connectors for Slack, Microsoft Outlook (Email/Calendar), Microsoft Teams (Calls/Chats), and Fathom (Meeting Transcripts).
- **Personal Knowledge Graph:** Mapping relationships between people, projects, and communication channels.
- **Episodic & Semantic Memory:** Ability to recall specific events (when) and the meaning/context of information (what).

### 2. Operational Assistance (The Executor)
- **Daily Briefing:** Morning summaries of meetings, reminders of unfulfilled promises, and priority tasks.
- **Automatic Action Item Tracking:** Extraction of commitments from transcripts and chats into a structured task list.
- **Gap Analysis:** Detecting contradictions or omissions across different communication channels.
- **Style-Based Ghostwriting:** Drafting responses that mirror the user's specific tone, vocabulary, and heuristics.

### 3. Learning & Evolution (The Digital Twin)
- **Behavioral Pattern Analysis:** Learning decision-making heuristics and prioritization logic.
- **Personality Modeling:** Filtering noise and handling trivial interactions autonomously based on user profile.
- **Opportunity Detection:** Suggesting process improvements based on interaction trends.

### 4. Interaction Layer
- **Conversational Voice Interface:** Low-latency STT/TTS for hands-free interaction.
- **Omnipresent Chat Interface:** Complex querying across the entire knowledge base.

## Strategic Pillars
- **Contextual Awareness:** The system should always know the "current state" of any professional relationship or project.
- **Proactive Nature:** Moving from a reactive "ask-and-answer" bot to a proactive "alert-and-suggest" assistant.
- **Privacy-First:** Granular control over what is stored and what is ignored.
